Kraina Media

KRAINA Media is a Belarus-centered media that brings the experience of other countries in reformation to the Belarusian audience as well as brings up discussions around Belarusian culture (in a very broad meaning), history, and Identity.

Target audience: Active minority, Belarusians, who are driving social transformations: 25-40, proactive, intelligent, open-minded, have seen the world and share democratic values.
