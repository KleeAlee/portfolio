it was not my choice

This series is about the place where I grew up — a landscape I didn't choose, but one that left its mark on me anyway.

Visually, it’s a space caught between heavy concrete structures and quiet, fading memories, carrying an atmosphere that felt both grounding and suffocating. My relationship with it has always been mixed: affection paired with genuine frustration. Most of the physical backdrop from those years has changed or disappeared, but its imprint remains. These images look at what lingers in the gaps, and question whether you ever completely leave your hometown behind.
