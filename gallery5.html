Photographer
somewhere not home

This series of black-and-white photographs explores the layered nature of perception. Behind each clear, visible surface, something else flickers—unfamiliar, blurred, perhaps intriguing, perhaps unsettling. We don't fully understand it, but we are drawn to look closer. New forms emerge: objects half-recognised, fragments of presence or absence, traces of what might have been. The images invite curiosity, asking not just what we see, but what lies just beyond sight.
