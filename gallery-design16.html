Kafka-Codex Logotype Design

"Kafka-Codex". One of the projects by "Novaya Gazeta". This is a story about the most absurd court cases in Russia in recent years: not political cases, ordinary ones, about the ordinary people. For example "The case about the rehabilitation of an innocent" or "The case about illegal nuts". The materials contains a video commentary by an lawyer, and a comic book that tells about the essence of the case.
