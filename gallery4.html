This series captures the beauty of chaos—how things simply happen, or nearly happen, as the culmination of countless small actions. It speaks to chance and unpredictability, an endless wellspring of inspiration.

It’s about finding harmony within the chaos, a natural order that emerges without intention. There’s no need to intervene—just observe, take notes, and let the randomness unfold. In this chaotic harmony, you discover a raw, unfiltered beauty that invites reflection and wonder.
