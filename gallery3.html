urban vision

A series of black and white film photographs capturing the essence of the city and its myriad stories. Each shot unfolds like a narrative, provoking its own set of questions: Why is everything the way it is? Why did this happen, and how did it come to be? What chain of events led to this moment?

As you gaze upon these images, you wonder — are you the only one who perceives these nuances, or are they visible to all? Each photograph invites reflection, drawing you deeper into the mysteries that lie within the city's soul.
